## Safety and evidence status of the existing prototype

The existing quiz and seller prompt are **demonstrations, not production advice**. They contain hard-coded assumptions about costs, payback, subsidies, stock, and configurations. Before customer use they require:

- live product and inventory data;
- compatibility rules approved by the technical team;
- source-dated tariff, tax, and subsidy data;
- a validated calculation engine with visible assumptions;
- human approval for quotes and availability;
- removal of any unsupported “exact” recommendation or guaranteed outcome.

The BMF confirms the German PV VAT zero-rate framework and its conditions, but eligibility depends on the transaction and installation context. It must not be reduced to an unconditional “0% VAT for everything” claim: <https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/FAQ/foerderung-photovoltaikanlagen.html>.

## Source-quality rule

Maintain three labels in future research:

- **Verified public fact** — visible on an official company/government/source page, with URL and review date.
- **Internal fact requiring owner confirmation** — supplied by Frank or family but not public.
- **Hypothesis** — product, market, pricing, naming, or operational idea to test.

Never promote a hypothesis into customer-facing copy merely because an earlier agent wrote it confidently.
