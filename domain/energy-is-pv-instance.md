# Energy IS PV Domain Instance (Sovereign for PV Lager / Rial)

This is the application of the Energy IS Domain Sub-Stack (from Starlight Intelligence System) to the PV Lager alliance.

## PV-Specific Domain Concepts
- **PV Carport Project**: Customer/project entity with learning mode, jurisdiction, site evidence, structure route, human gates, exact product versions, and decision receipts.
- **Zola Car Pod**: Unverified working/internal label retained only for historical searchability until ownership and public-use status are confirmed.
- **Hybrid Climate Kit**: AC/DC PV Multi-Split configurations (BTU combos, action pricing, self-consumption boost).
- **Structural PV**: Terrassen, Zäune, Vordächer as first-class products with architectural params.
- **Local Fulfillment Profile**: Seesen warehouse, pickup windows, immediate availability flag.
- **German Rules Profile**: source-dated tax, tariff, grid, registration, and funding facts with jurisdiction, effective date, reviewer, and explicit unknown/stale states.

## Sub-Systems (Energy IS 7)
1. Site and system evidence (no structural inference)
2. Source-dated scenarios (not unreviewed quotes or forecasts)
3. Buyer Clarity (readiness scan → project path and open gates)
4. Professional handoffs (planning, structural, electrical, supplier)
5. Operations (queue, freshness, incidents, inventory sources)
6. Grid / registration / rules and compliance
7. Service, maintenance, upgrade, recovery, and referral

Uses ValidationRequirement and decision receipts before any calculator or recommendation can be treated as decision-grade. The current Lab policy is in [`../labs/pv-carport-lab/`](../labs/pv-carport-lab/README.md).
